NIGERIA LIFE — FIRST PLAYABLE PROTOTYPE

How to run:
1. Open index.html in a modern browser.
2. On Android, open it in Chrome. For easiest use, extract the ZIP first and open index.html.
3. Use the on-screen direction pad to move. Drag on the world to rotate the camera.
4. WASD/arrow keys also work on a keyboard.

What is included:
- Self-contained browser prototype with no build tools or account required
- Lagos-inspired street, buildings, market stalls, simple traffic and pedestrians
- Walking controls and camera rotation interaction
- Day/night toggle and horn button

Important limitations:
This is an early 2.5D canvas prototype, not a finished full 3D game. It has no online multiplayer, accounts, real banking, persistent saves, or complete Nigeria map yet. Those require further development and appropriate hosting/backend services.
